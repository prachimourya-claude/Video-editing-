# Bali Diary 01 — Mumbai → Bali on Malaysia Airlines

Vertical 9:16, ~52s, for Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts. Hinglish voiceover.

## Voiceover script

| Time | Footage | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6s | Sunrise wing in window frame → route map BOM → KUL → DPS | Mumbai se Bali… via Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia Airlines pe. Chalo, dikhati hoon experience kaisa raha! |
| 6–15s | Boarding flight 1 → crew safety demo in batik kebaya | Raat ke 12 baje boarding. Flight full thi, red-purple seats… aur crew ki batik kebaya uniform — sach mein, Malaysia wali feel aa gayi. |
| 15–26s | Meal: tray, foil, paratha, rice-curry | Raat 1 baje dinner. Maine veg liya, husband ne non-veg. Paratha, rice-curry, salad, Amul dahi… veg meal bas theek-thaak tha. |
| 26–30s | Old seatback screen, finger on remote | Aur screens? Humne kaafi expect kiya tha… but honestly, letdown tha. |
| 30–37s | Sunrise wing, full screen (hold 2s silence first) | …lekin phir subah ye view mila. |
| 37–47s | Map: plane lands at KUL → flight 2 cabin | KL mein chhota sa layover, aur doosri flight Bali ke liye — ekdum upgrade! Naya plane, bade screens, fresh cabin. |
| 47–52s | Paper waves, sun rises, "Next stop: Bali" | Next stop… Bali! Baaki trip ke liye follow karte raho. |
