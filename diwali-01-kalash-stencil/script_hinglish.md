## Storyboard

1. **Hook (0–3s)**: start mid-motion, punch from 100% → 115% on the stencil, golden light leak and hand-drawn diya doodles in the corners. Big text over the top third: "Rangoli in 5 minutes? 🪔".
2. **Problem (3–8s)**: printer shot, a bit desaturated. Strike-through animation on the text "same old designs".
3. **Reveal (8–14s)**: colour comes back with a soft flash at the voice line "khud design kiya". Bambu printer move → match cut on the lotus shapes being printed. Small badge: "Made in-house · 3D printed".
4. **Product hero (14–20s)**: 0.6× slow-mo on the hand-held turn. Thin gold arrows draw onto the kalash ("Kalash = shubh aarambh") and the lotus ("Kamal = Lakshmi ji"). A gentle glow on the stencil edges.
5. **How to use (20–25s)**: three quick cuts: stencil laid on the floor → colour poured into the sections → stencil lifted to show the finished rangoli (money shot, hold 1.5s). Number chips 1·2·3 pop in turn.
6. **CTA (25–30s)**: curtain shot, warm grade, sparkles falling, brand handle + "DM to order" pill. End card holds 1s so the loop back to the hook feels clean.

Visual style: festive warm grade (marigold, maroon, gold), cream paper captions to match the existing reels, Devanagari accents only for "शुभ दीपावली".
Music: light festive instrumental (shehnai/sitar + soft percussion), ducked under the voice. Royalty-free; credit goes in the caption.

### Shot to film (strongly recommended)

None of the clips show the stencil **in use**, and that's the shot that sells it. A 10–15s vertical clip, top-down, would make the reel:
1. Lay the stencil on the floor or a tile (2s)
2. Fill 2–3 sections with rangoli colour, sped up in the edit (6s)
3. Slowly lift the stencil straight up to show the finished kalash (3s, keep the camera steady)
