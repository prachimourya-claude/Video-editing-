# 03 — Instagram Marketing & Sales

**Goal for 15 days:** turn reels into DMs, and DMs into paid orders on WhatsApp.
**Your unfair advantage:** 3D printing timelapses are some of the most watchable content on Instagram. Show the machine making the product.

## Content pillars (rotate them)

1. **Making-of**: timelapses, the nozzle close-up, "how long does this take?". This brings reach.
2. **Glow shots**: the lantern switched on, patterns on the wall, diya rows. This brings desire.
3. **Personalisation**: "We printed your name". Type a name, then reveal the print. This brings DMs.
4. **Proof**: packing orders, customer photos, "50 keychains for XYZ Pvt Ltd". This builds trust.
5. **Offer + urgency**: prices, hampers, "last day to order". This brings sales.

## Reel hooks (first 1–2 seconds decide everything)

- "6 ghante, 312 layers… ek lantern." (timelapse → switch-on)
- "Ye diya jalta nahi… phir bhi roshni deta hai." (LED reveal)
- "Comment your name, hum print karenge." (personalisation, pick 3 names from comments)
- "Rangoli banana nahi aata? Ye try karo." (stencil + colour pour; the satisfying kind of content)
- "Diwali gift under ₹700 jo mithai nahi hai."
- "POV: tumhara brand logo, 100 keychains mein." (corporate)
- "Mummy ne kaha *ye toh shop se laaya hoga*." (family reaction)

Every reel: 9:16, 12–25 s, trending audio or soft festive instrumental, **text on screen** (most people watch on mute), and the last frame says *"DM 'DIWALI' to order"*.

## Paid ads (small budget, high focus)

- **Budget:** ₹300–500/day from 26 Oct to 3 Nov (₹3–4.5k total). Boost only reels that already did well organically after 24 hours.
- **Objective:** "Messages" (WhatsApp/Instagram DM), not "Profile visits".
- **Audience A, gifting:** India, age 24–45, interests: home décor, Diwali, gifts, Pinterest, interior design. Metro + tier-1 cities.
- **Audience B, local:** 15–25 km around your city, for same-city delivery and walk-in/bulk.
- **Audience C, NRIs:** Instagram users in UAE/US/UK with interest "India" sending gifts home (ship to an Indian address).
- Stop any ad that costs more than ~₹40 per conversation after ₹500 spent.

## Sales system: DM → paid order in 5 messages

Set up **WhatsApp Business**: catalogue with every product + price, a greeting message, an away message, quick replies (`/price`, `/order`, `/ship`, `/bulk`), and labels (`New`, `Paid`, `Printing`, `Shipped`).

**On Instagram,** set an auto-reply for the keywords `DIWALI` and `BULK` (Instagram's built-in automated responses, or ManyChat/Zorcha) that sends the WhatsApp link + catalogue.

**The flow**
1. **Greet + show:** "Hi! 🪔 Thanks for reaching out. Here's our Diwali collection 👉 [catalogue]. What are you looking for: home décor, a gift, or bulk?"
2. **Recommend:** suggest 1 product + 1 hamper. Don't send 10 options.
3. **Personalise + confirm:** name/colour → "Here's a quick mock-up". A Canva preview or a slicer screenshot closes sales.
4. **Pay:** "Total ₹___ incl. shipping. Pay via UPI ___ or this link [Razorpay / Instamojo]. Orders are printed after payment." Ask for **100% prepaid** on retail orders; avoid COD for personalised items.
5. **Update:** photo of their item on the printer ("Your lantern is printing! 🔥"), then the tracking link. These photos turn into reposts and reviews.

**Policies to post in the "How to Order" highlight**
- Made to order: dispatch in 2–4 days. Order by **1 Nov** for courier delivery before Diwali.
- Personalised items: no returns. Breakage in transit: send an unboxing video for a free reprint.
- Use with the included LED tealight only. Not for real flames.
