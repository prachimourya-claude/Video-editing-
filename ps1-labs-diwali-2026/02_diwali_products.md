# 02 — The Diwali Collection: "Roshni by PSOne Labs"

**Your setup:** one **Bambu Lab A1** printing in **PLA**. That makes **printer time your scarcest resource.** With 15 days left, sell **few products, done well**: one hero you already have (the elephant), two quick prints, one personalised item and one bulk item. Gift boxes raise order value without new designs.

> ⚠️ **Safety rule for every lighting product: LED tealights only, never a real flame.** PLA softens at about 55–60 °C, so a burning candle will warp or melt it and can catch fire. Put "Use with the included LED tealight only" on the product card and the box. Buy warm-flicker LED tealights in bulk (roughly ₹8–15 each wholesale; Crawford Market / Lohar Chawl or online) and include them.
>
> **Incense holder:** fine in PLA, because the stick burns out before the ember reaches the holder. Add a small **ash tray/plate** under the elephant (print it, or sell it with a brass plate) so hot ash never sits on the PLA or the customer's shelf.

## Pricing formula (use it for every quote)

```
Material      = grams used × (₹ per kg ÷ 1000)          e.g. 120 g × ₹1.1 = ₹132
Machine time  = print hours × ₹20–30/hour                (power + wear + paying off the A1)
Add-ons       = LED tealight / ash plate / keyring / cork + box + sticker + card
Failure buffer= +10% of the above
─────────────────────────────────────────
Cost          = sum of the above
Retail price  = Cost × 2.5 to 3                           (round to ₹_99)
Bulk price    = Cost × 1.6 to 2                           (by quantity tier, see file 04)
```

Worked example, Diya Set of 4:
- First version: 4 × 35 g = 140 g PLA (₹155) + 3.5 h × ₹25 (₹88) + 4 LEDs (₹50) + box/sticker/card (₹45) = ₹338, plus 10% = **₹372 cost**. At ×2.5 that's ₹930, too high for a diya set.
- Cheaper version: lighter 25 g shells, all 4 on one plate, bulk LEDs: 100 g (₹110) + 2.5 h × ₹25 (₹63) + LEDs (₹40) + packaging (₹35) = ₹248, plus 10% = **₹273 cost → ₹699 retail** (×2.6). ✅

Do this maths for every product before you post prices. Bambu Studio shows the real grams and print time after slicing.

Courier cost: Shiprocket/Delhivery charge roughly ₹60–120 per 0.5 kg within India. Either price it in ("Free shipping above ₹999") or charge it at checkout. Within Mumbai, use Porter / Dunzo-style same-day delivery or hand-deliver.

## Capacity: the number that decides everything

One A1 running about **20 h/day for the 10 selling days = about 200 printer-hours** until Diwali. That's your whole budget. Plan it:

| Product | Hours/pc | Per day (if only this) | Suggested Diwali cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Elephant | ~4 | ~5 | **30** (≈120 h) |
| Diya set of 4 | ~2.5 | ~8 | **20 sets** (≈50 h) |
| Keychains | ~0.2 | ~100 | bulk orders, fill the gaps |
| Nameplate | ~2.5 | ~8 | **10** (≈25 h) |

That already adds up to about 200 h, so:
- **Announce limited quantities**: "Only 30 Diwali elephants." Scarcity sells and protects your one printer.
- **Bulk orders = keychains** (and a few coaster sets). Never accept 50 elephants for Diwali delivery; offer them for **wedding season / New Year** delivery instead.
- **Print overnight**: queue long prints (elephant, nameplate) for the night, and short plates (keychains, diyas) for the day when you can swap plates.
- Keep a **print queue** in a Google Sheet: order ID · item · colour · name · status · dispatch date.
- **Backup:** find one Mumbai 3D printing service or a friend with a Bambu printer now, at an agreed per-gram rate, for overflow. Even better, if Diwali goes well, a **second A1** pays for itself after roughly 60–80 orders.
